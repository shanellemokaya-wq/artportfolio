POLICIES

Shipping, returns, privacy and shop information.

SHIPPING

Prints are made to order and shipped to the UK and selected international destinations. Available destinations are shown at checkout.

Shipping costs are calculated based on your destination, print size and format, and are shown before payment. Delivery times vary by destination and are estimates rather than guaranteed dates.

International orders may be subject to local customs duties or taxes, which are the responsibility of the recipient.

RETURNS & REFUNDS

If you change your mind, please contact me within 14 days of receiving your order. Returned prints must be sent back within 14 days of notifying me. Return postage is the responsibility of the customer.

Where a cancellation is accepted, any refund due will be issued within 14 days of receiving the returned goods. Standard delivery charges paid with the original order will also be refunded where required by law.

If your order arrives damaged, faulty or incorrect, please contact me with your order details and photographs of the issue so I can arrange a replacement or refund where appropriate.

PRIVACY

Personal information provided when placing an order, such as your name, email address, delivery address and order details, is used only to process, fulfil and communicate with you about your order.

Information is shared only where necessary to provide these services, including with Stripe for payment processing, Printseekers for order fulfilment and delivery, and Resend for transactional order emails. I do not use your information for marketing.

Personal information is kept only for as long as necessary for the purposes described above and to meet any legal, tax or accounting obligations.

You have rights over your personal information under UK data protection law. To ask about your information or exercise your rights, please contact me at shanellemokaya@gmail.com.

You also have the right to raise a concern with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

TERMS

By placing an order, you agree to the prices, shipping costs and product details shown at checkout. Orders are confirmed once payment has been successfully processed.
